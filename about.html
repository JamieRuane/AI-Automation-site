About Ampel

Making Intelligent Automation Accessible to Every Business.

Ampel is an emerging automation consultancy working with small and medium-sized businesses in Ireland and the UK.

Our idea

Time Is Better Spent on the Work Only You Can Do.

Ampel was founded around a simple idea: businesses should not need to waste valuable time on repetitive administrative processes that technology can handle.

We design, build, implement and maintain automation systems that connect your business tools, simplify repetitive processes and help your team work more efficiently.

Our approach is practical, transparent and focused on measurable improvements. We agree what success looks like before we build, we tell you plainly what automation can and cannot do, and we avoid promising specific savings.

What we combine

Five Disciplines, One Working System.

AI technology

Language models for reading, classifying and drafting.

Workflow automation

Reliable triggers, steps and error handling.

API integrations

Connecting the software you already use.

Software engineering

Built, tested and documented properly.

Process optimisation

Fixing the process before automating it.

How we work

Plain Language, Clear Scope.

We start with your process. Automation built on a confusing process just makes the confusion faster, so we look at how the work happens today first.

We build around your tools. You keep the software your team already knows, and we connect it.

We keep people in the loop. Where AI drafts or decides something important, a person reviews it.

We document what we build. You receive a handover so the system is never a black box.
