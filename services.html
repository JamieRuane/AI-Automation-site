Lead Management Automation

Automatically capture, organise and manage incoming customer enquiries.

The problem

Enquiries arrive through a website form, an inbox and the odd phone message. Some are answered late, some are forgotten, and nobody has a single view of who is waiting for a reply.

The solution

Every enquiry is captured in one place, tagged by type and urgency, added to your CRM or spreadsheet, and passed to the right person. A reminder is scheduled so follow-up does not depend on someone remembering.

How the workflow operates

  1. Enquiry received

    Website form or email arrives

  2. Categorised

    Type, urgency and source tagged

  3. Added to CRM

    Contact and details recorded

  4. Team notified

    Right person alerted by email or chat

  5. Follow-up set

    Reminder created if no reply

Key features

  • Website enquiry capture
  • Automatic lead categorisation
  • CRM integration
  • Email notifications
  • Follow-up reminders
  • Lead tracking

Business benefits

  • Fewer enquiries lost between inboxes and notes
  • Consistent follow-up that does not rely on memory
  • A single, current view of your pipeline

Example use cases

  • A tradesperson logging quote requests from their website
  • An estate agent routing viewing requests to the right negotiator
  • An agency capturing inbound leads from several forms

AI Email Automation

Use AI to organise incoming emails, identify important enquiries and prepare responses.

The problem

A shared inbox fills up with a mix of enquiries, invoices, spam and updates. Important messages get buried, and the same replies are typed out again and again.

The solution

AI reads each incoming email, labels it, pulls out key details such as names, dates and order numbers, and routes it to the right person. Where it helps, it drafts a reply for a human to review and send.

How the workflow operates

  1. Email arrives

    Shared inbox or support address

  2. AI classifies

    Enquiry, invoice, support, other

  3. Details extracted

    Names, dates, references

  4. Routed and flagged

    Priority alert to the right team

  5. Reply drafted

    Held for human review

Key features

  • Email classification
  • Information extraction
  • AI-assisted response drafting
  • Priority notifications
  • Department routing
  • Email workflow automation

Business benefits

  • Faster triage of what needs attention first
  • Replies stay under human control before sending
  • More consistent tone and information in responses

Example use cases

  • A shared info@ inbox sorted into sales, support and admin
  • A recruitment agency pulling details from candidate emails
  • An accountancy practice flagging client emails with attachments

Document Processing Automation

Turn repetitive document handling into automated digital workflows.

The problem

PDFs, invoices and forms arrive by email or upload, and someone retypes the details into a spreadsheet or system, then renames and files each one by hand.

The solution

Documents are picked up automatically, key fields are extracted, each file is categorised and renamed, and the data is exported in a structured format. Anything the system is unsure about is flagged for a person to check.

How the workflow operates

  1. Document received

    Email attachment or upload folder

  2. Fields extracted

    Supplier, date, totals, reference

  3. Categorised

    Document type identified

  4. Checked

    Unclear items flagged for review

  5. Exported and filed

    Sheet row added, file organised

Key features

  • PDF information extraction
  • Invoice data extraction
  • Document categorisation
  • Structured data export
  • Automated document organisation

Business benefits

  • Less retyping of figures from documents
  • Files named and stored in a consistent structure
  • Uncertain extractions surfaced instead of hidden

Example use cases

  • Supplier invoices extracted into a spreadsheet for bookkeeping
  • CVs summarised into a candidate tracker
  • Client documents sorted into the right folders

AI Customer Support

Create intelligent customer support systems that answer common questions and route complicated enquiries to human employees.

The problem

The same questions come in repeatedly, and enquiries sent outside working hours wait until someone is back at their desk.

The solution

A chat assistant on your website answers common questions using information you have approved, collects details for anything it cannot answer, and hands the conversation to a person with the context attached.

How the workflow operates

  1. Visitor asks

    Question typed on your website

  2. Checks your information

    Only your approved content is used

  3. Answers or collects

    Replies, or takes contact details

  4. Escalates to a person

    Complex enquiries passed on

  5. Logged

    Conversation saved for review

Key features

  • Website chatbots
  • Business knowledge integration
  • FAQ automation
  • Enquiry collection
  • Human escalation

Business benefits

  • Common questions answered at any hour
  • Complicated enquiries reach a person with details attached
  • Conversation logs show what customers actually ask

Example use cases

  • Opening hours, service area and pricing questions
  • Collecting details for a callback or quote
  • Order status and returns questions for an online shop

Automated Business Reporting

Automatically collect, analyse and distribute business performance reports.

The problem

Weekly and monthly reports are assembled by copying figures between tools. It takes time, it is easy to make a mistake, and the report is often late.

The solution

Data is collected from your sheets and business tools on a schedule, combined into a consistent report, and sent to the people who need it. AI can add a plain-English summary and flag unusual changes.

How the workflow operates

  1. Scheduled run

    Weekly, monthly or on demand

  2. Data collected

    Sheets, CRM, forms and other tools

  3. Figures compiled

    Totals and comparisons calculated

  4. Summary written

    Plain-English overview drafted

  5. Report delivered

    Sent by email or posted to chat

Key features

  • Data collection
  • Spreadsheet integration
  • Automated reporting
  • AI-generated summaries
  • Performance notifications

Business benefits

  • Reports arrive on schedule without manual assembly
  • The same method every time, so figures are comparable
  • Alerts when a number moves outside a range you set

Example use cases

  • A weekly lead and sales summary for a small team
  • Client performance reports for a marketing agency
  • A daily orders digest for an online shop
