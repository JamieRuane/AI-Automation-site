FAQ

Questions Businesses Ask Us.

Straight answers to the most common questions. If yours isn't here, ask us in a free consultation.

Ask a question
What is business automation?
Business automation means using software to carry out repetitive tasks that a person would otherwise do step by step, such as moving enquiries into a CRM, sorting emails or pulling figures from invoices. Your team stays in charge of decisions and exceptions.
Does my business need AI?
Not always. Many tasks only need rules and connections between the tools you already use. We add AI where it genuinely helps, for example reading emails, extracting data from documents or drafting replies, and we will tell you when a simpler approach is enough.
Can FlowNet integrate with my existing software?
Often, yes. We build around tools such as Gmail, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack and Google Sheets, and many CRM systems. What is possible depends on what each tool offers, so we check this with you before any work starts.
How long does implementation take?
It depends on the scope. A single, straightforward workflow takes much less time than a connected system with several integrations. We give you a realistic estimate after the discovery conversation.
Do I need technical knowledge?
No. We handle the setup, test it with you and explain how it works in plain language. You also receive documentation, so you are not left guessing how the automation runs.
What happens if an automation stops working?
We build in error handling and notifications so a failure is flagged instead of going unnoticed. Changes in third-party tools can sometimes affect a workflow, which is why we offer optional maintenance plans. Without a plan, fixes can be arranged on request.
How much does automation cost?
Projects start from €350 for one straightforward workflow, from €750 for several connected steps and from €1,500 for a larger custom system. Final pricing depends on complexity, integrations and requirements. See the pricing page for what each package includes.
Do you provide ongoing maintenance?
Yes, as an option. Essential Care is €49 a month, Business Care is €99 a month and Custom Care starts from €199 a month. Third-party subscriptions and usage fees are billed separately by those providers.
Is my business data secure?
We only ask for the access a workflow needs, and we explain how your data moves between tools before we build anything, so you can meet your own data protection duties. No system is risk-free, so we recommend reviewing access with us as your needs change.
Can workflows be customised?
Yes. Every workflow is designed around the way your business already works and the software you already use, instead of being a fixed template.
