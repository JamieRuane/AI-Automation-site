Pricing questions

How Pricing Works.

How much does automation cost?
Projects start from €350 for one straightforward workflow, from €750 for several connected steps and from €1,500 for a larger custom system. Final pricing depends on complexity, integrations and requirements. See the pricing page for what each package includes.
Do you provide ongoing maintenance?
Yes, as an option. Essential Care is €49 a month, Business Care is €99 a month and Custom Care starts from €199 a month. Third-party subscriptions and usage fees are billed separately by those providers.
How long does implementation take?
It depends on the scope. A single, straightforward workflow takes much less time than a connected system with several integrations. We give you a realistic estimate after the discovery conversation.
