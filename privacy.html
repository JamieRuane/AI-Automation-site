Privacy

Privacy Notice.

How FlowNet handles the information you send through this website.

What we collect

When you submit the consultation form we receive the details you enter: your name, business name, business email, optional website, industry, team size, a description of your automation challenge, current tools, estimated budget and any additional information. We also record the time of submission.

Why we collect it

We use these details only to reply to your enquiry, to assess whether automation fits your business, and to keep a record of the conversation. You must tick the consent box before sending the form.

Who sees it

Enquiries are delivered to FlowNet through the system that receives the form. They are not displayed publicly. We do not sell your information. We may use tools such as email, spreadsheets or a CRM to manage enquiries, and those providers process the data on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond, to follow up on a project you ask about, and to meet reasonable business record-keeping needs. You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes, this notice will be updated and consent requested where required.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and you can object to its use. To make a request, send an enquiry through the contact form and say it is a privacy request. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland or the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the business and website develop. The current version is always on this page.
